BasketBoss — Dinner sorted.
APP STORE HEADER AND SEARCH RESULTS ARTWORK

QUICK START
1. Unzip the downloaded bundle on your Mac.
2. In App Store Connect > BasketBoss > Distribution > iOS App 1.0 > Product Page Information > Header, upload BasketBoss-Header-3840x1646.png.
3. In the Search Results tab, upload BasketBoss-Search-Results-3840x2560.png.
4. Use Preview to check Apple's crops, then Save.

ALTERNATIVE: ONE FILE FOR BOTH PLACEMENTS
Use BasketBoss-Universal-5244x2950.png as the Header asset and select "Use header asset in search results" if that option is shown. You do not need all three files in a single placement; each is a correctly sized alternative for the placement in its filename.

These files are marketing artwork, separate from the nine actual iPhone screenshots and the App Preview video. Upload the iPhone screenshots in the appropriate iPhone screenshot size, not in Header.

DESIGN AND EXPORT
The composition was generated using existing BasketBoss recipe photographs (fajitas, chickpea/spinach curry, creamy chicken pasta) and the crowned basket icon as references. The master is 1672x941 pixels; the supplied exports are centre-cropped/resampled to Apple's exact upload sizes. PNGs are opaque, 8-bit RGB/sRGB with no alpha channel. The app and main branch have not been changed. Keep essential branding in view when checking the preview, as Apple's displayed crops vary.
