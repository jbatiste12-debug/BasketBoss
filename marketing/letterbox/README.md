# BasketBoss letterbox flyer

A single-sided A5 flyer matching the website’s forest green, cream and gold design.

Headline: **Shop your kitchen before the supermarket.**

## Print files

- `BasketBoss-A5-Flyer.pdf`: 148 × 210 mm, for printing at 100% / actual size.
- `BasketBoss-A5-Flyer-3mm-Bleed.pdf`: 154 × 216 mm media, A5 TrimBox, 3 mm bleed on all sides. Supply this file to a commercial printer; crop marks can be added during imposition.
- `BasketBoss-A5-Flyer.png`: 300 dpi, 1749 × 2481 pixels.
- `BasketBoss-A5-Flyer-preview.png`: smaller screen preview.

Suggested stock: 170 gsm matte, printed single-sided.

## QR code

The QR opens **https://basketboss.co.uk/download**. The existing website route sends iPhone and iPad users to BasketBoss on the App Store, and other devices to website features.

The 34 mm QR includes its white quiet zone. The final PNG and both PDFs rendered at 300, 150 and 96 dpi all decoded to the exact destination: seven checks passed. See `final-qr-verification.json` for dimensions and tested file hashes.

## Artwork

Uses the real BasketBoss icon and example meal-plan screen, the unchanged official App Store badge, a supporting food photograph, and vector text and QR artwork. The example plan contains demonstration data. Copy offers 100 free recipes and identifies Premium as optional.
