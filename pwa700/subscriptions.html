Seven-day Founding Member trial

Eligible Founding Members can try Premium for seven days. Paddle takes payment details when the trial starts, but the subscription fee is scheduled for after the trial. If you do not cancel before that charge becomes due, the subscription automatically renews at £1.99 per month.

Check the exact trial end, first charge and billing schedule displayed by Paddle; a trial is not established until confirmed in Paddle checkout. Standard Premium is advertised with a three-day free trial, followed by £4.99/month; confirm exact dates and totals in checkout.

Manage or cancel in the app

  1. Sign in to BasketBoss using the account that purchased Premium.
  2. Open Settings → Premium membership → Manage subscription / cancel.
  3. The secure Paddle Customer Portal shows your subscription, payment method and cancellation options.
  4. Follow Paddle's confirmation steps and keep the cancellation confirmation for your records.

You can also use the Manage subscription link in the original Paddle receipt, or visit paddle.net. You do not need to email BasketBoss merely to cancel.

Cancellation usually takes effect at the end of the current billing period (or the trial period, where applicable), preventing further renewal charges. Your exact access end date is shown in Paddle's confirmation and reflected by BasketBoss after a verified subscription update. If you cancel during the seven-day trial before the first charge, no recurring fee should be charged for that trial.

Refunds and consumer rights

Canceling and requesting a refund are different actions. A cancellation prevents future renewals; it does not automatically refund an already collected payment.

Paddle acts as the merchant of record for subscription transactions and handles eligible refunds. To request one, follow the support link in your Paddle receipt or visit paddle.net.

Read Paddle's current Refund Policy for eligibility, how to request a refund, regional withdrawal rights and discretionary refund handling. Applicable statutory consumer rights are not reduced or excluded by this page.

Apple subscriptions in the iOS app

Where enabled, choose Premium Monthly or Premium Annual using Apple’s purchase sheet. The intended UK prices are £4.99/month or £39.99/year paid upfront; Apple displays the actual local price before you confirm. These plans renew automatically until cancelled. Any Apple trial or offer must appear in the Apple purchase sheet; the web trials above do not automatically apply.

Use the same BasketBoss account for the original purchase and Restore Purchases. Existing eligible web subscribers can sign in to the iPhone app without subscribing again. Premium access is granted only after server verification of an eligible purchase.

Manage or cancel Apple billing through Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions or Apple subscription settings. Cancellation prevents future renewals; access normally continues to the verified paid-through date. Apple handles refund requests at reportaproblem.apple.com; a refund is separate from cancellation. Deleting your BasketBoss account does not cancel Apple billing.

The £1.99 Founding Member plan is available on Apple only if explicitly enabled and offered in the app under verified eligibility terms. Creating a product in App Store Connect does not make the offer available.

Access, payment problems and support

Premium benefits begin only after our billing system verifies an eligible Apple purchase, an eligible trialing or active Paddle subscription, or verifies an authorised complimentary Owner account. If a payment fails or a subscription ends, Premium access may be paused or removed; Free features remain accessible.

Premium AI tools, where available, may be subject to the displayed daily usage limits, technical availability and fair-use protections. Trial and billing status never bypass those server-side controls.

For invoice, charge, refund or cancellation help, use the Paddle receipt or paddle.net. For app functionality and account issues, email support@basketboss.co.uk.
