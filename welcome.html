GOOD TO KNOW

A few answers.

Built for your family’s week.

How can BasketBoss help me spend less?

Start with food already at home and use your plan to buy what’s missing. Savings depend on your household, shopping and food waste.

Is BasketBoss free?

Yes. Free includes 200 recipes and the everyday basics. Confirmed Free accounts also get one new advanced shopping trip each week. Premium is optional.

How does stock update?

Finish Shop adds confirmed purchases. Confirm cooked deducts ingredients once. Check quantities and manually review changes, leftovers and food safety.

Can I install it on my phone?

Yes. Use BasketBoss on the web or add it to your Home Screen from a compatible browser. An iPhone and iPad app is also available on the App Store; see the recipe availability note above.
