Buy a pack. Use what you need. Keep the rest.

Fresh food requires safe-storage confirmation. Unmeasured or unconfirmed pantry items never count as unlimited stock.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions parents ask.

BasketBoss is in Beta. We're focused on making the features already here more reliable and useful.

Is BasketBoss free?

Yes. Start with 100 free recipes and basic planning. Premium adds extra tools and household partner sharing.

Does the example week change my meal plan?

No. This landing page only demonstrates publicly available recipes. Your actual saved plans are changed only inside the signed-in app.

Can I share with a partner?

Premium supports one verified partner per paid household. A partner accepts an invitation through their own confirmed BasketBoss account.

Can I install BasketBoss?

Yes. Open the full app in a compatible browser and follow its install instructions to add it to your home screen.
