GOOD TO KNOW

A few answers.

BasketBoss is currently in Beta.

Is the £25+ saving guaranteed?

No. It’s an example weekly target, not a measured result or a guarantee. Your savings depend on your household, shopping and food waste.

Is BasketBoss free?

Yes. Free includes 100 recipes and the everyday basics. Confirmed Free accounts also get one new advanced shopping trip each week. Premium is optional.

How does stock update?

Finish Shop adds confirmed purchases. Confirm cooked deducts ingredients once. Check quantities and manually review changes, leftovers and food safety.

Can I install it on my phone?

Yes. Open the app in a compatible browser and follow its home-screen installation steps.
