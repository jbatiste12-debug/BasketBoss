A SIMPLE RHYTHM FOR THE WEEK

Plan. Restock. Cook.
Make the most of what’s left.

Your pantry is a picture of what you have at home. It carries on from one week to the next.

  1. 01
    Plan your mealsChoose dinners, lunches and desserts. Auto-fill can favour ingredients you already have and bring variety to the week.
  2. 02
    Check At homeUpdate the amounts in Cupboard, Fridge and Freezer. Your Shop list adjusts as valid quantities are saved.
  3. 03
    Shop for what’s missingAdd any extra items you need. Finish Shop adds confirmed purchases to your stock, including the full pack you bought.
  4. 04
    Cook, then carry onConfirm cooked to deduct a meal, or mark Day done for the remaining planned food that day. Check what’s left before next week.

GOOD TO KNOW

Good questions.
Simple answers.

The essentials about Free, Premium and how your kitchen stays connected. BasketBoss is currently in Beta.

Is BasketBoss free?

Yes. Start with 100 free recipes, basic planning, At home stock checks and unlimited basic shopping lists. Sign in for one new advanced shopping trip each week; Premium includes unlimited advanced trips, all 500 recipes and extra tools. Reopening the same unfinished trip does not use another allowance. The Free week resets on Monday at 00:00 UTC.

How do I avoid buying food I already have?

Plan your meals, open At home and enter the amounts you can confirm. Tap a food tile and save the amount you have. Valid edits update the live Shop shortfall automatically. Food requirements come from your plan; only household Weekly essentials have a manual target. Purchases add to your pantry, and confirmed cooking deducts what you use.

Does the example week change my meal plan?

No. This landing page only demonstrates publicly available recipes. Your actual saved plans are changed only inside the signed-in app.

How are shopping prices estimated?

Choose your region and supermarket. Quantities scale for your meals, compatible ingredients are combined, confirmed pantry amounts are taken into account, then shopping packs are counted. Dated online guides cover selected UK products and regional pack estimates fill gaps. You can save your usual product and price. These are estimates; check current shop prices. Unknown Essentials prices show “Price needed”.

Will it remember my receipt for next week?

After you review and confirm a receipt, approved food quantities are saved in your pantry. Confirm what remains before your next shop, then those amounts can cover ingredients in your next meal plan. Reviewed regular prices help future estimates for matching products, pack sizes, regions and supermarkets. Household goods stay separate from food stock. Scanning is optional Premium early access, with up to 5 photo scans a day, subject to availability.

Can I leave out ingredients we don’t eat?

Yes. Open Plan → Ingredients to avoid, search the food tiles and choose what to leave out of future Auto-fill. Your manually chosen meals stay in the plan. The picker stays tucked away until you tap it. See the ingredient picker guide.

What if I’m too tired for tonight’s dinner?

With Premium, expand tonight’s meal in Plan and tap Too tired / short on time? below Cook. BasketBoss checks for a quicker dinner already planned later this week that your confirmed PANTRY quantities can cover. Review both meals and the postponed fresh ingredients’ packaging use-by dates, then confirm the swap. Both meals keep their servings and the week’s recipe quantities. See how the dinner swap works.

Can I use my voice while cooking?

Yes, with Premium Cook Mentor. Turn mentor voice on, ask a question about your active recipe and follow up naturally. You can also say “Next ingredient”, “Start cooking”, “Next step”, “Repeat” or “Start timer” for the available recipe controls. Spoken replies are AI-generated. Internet access, a supported device/browser and microphone or speech permissions are needed. You can stop listening or type instead. See the Voice Cook guide.

What happens to my pantry after cooking?

Confirm cooked deducts the meal’s ingredients once. Day done covers the remaining planned dinner, lunch and dessert for that day. Linked leftover meals do not deduct the original raw ingredients twice. Remaining stock carries into the next week; update the amounts when something changes.

Can I create a meal from a photo?

With Premium, photograph ingredients, review the recognised food and ask for a meal idea with an illustrative picture. Generated recipes and pictures may need checking and are not guarantees of dietary suitability. AI tools have daily limits and depend on service availability.

Can I share with a partner?

Premium supports one verified partner per paid household. A partner accepts an invitation through their own confirmed BasketBoss account.

Can I install BasketBoss?

Yes. Open the full app in a compatible browser and follow its install instructions to add it to your home screen.
