BasketBoss 1.0.4 (54) — phone checklist

1. Prepare and run
   Run the Build 54 launcher. Confirm version 1.0.4 and build 54 in the App target,
   connect your iPhone and choose Product > Run. Earlier projects stay intact.

2. Countable foods
   Open At home > Add food and choose Eggs using autocomplete. Confirm you see
   Got it / Running low / Out, How many left?, count buttons and one stepper.
   Pack-size suggestions, Full/Half pack and extra-pack instructions must be hidden.
   Tap 4 and Done. Reopen and confirm four eggs remain. Check an existing item via
   its Edit control receives the same simpler presentation.

3. Running low without interrogation
   With six eggs recorded, tap Running low. The suggested count should be two;
   adjust if needed, then tap Done immediately. Done confirms that displayed count.
   With one egg recorded, Running low must not increase it to two. Repeated taps
   must not add stock. Test a tin recorded with its container unit as well.

4. Got it without an amount
   Add a new food, choose Got it and Done without adding an amount. It should save
   once as unknown. A required recipe ingredient must keep the full shopping gap
   and show Check stock. Unknown must not become zero, a full pack or enough food.
   Tap Done twice quickly and confirm only one item is saved.

5. Measured pack shortcuts
   With rice and a reviewed 1 kg pack, tap Running low and Done without another
   confirmation. Confirm 250 g remains and the estimate is labelled.
   Open Add amount and check Full pack, Half pack and Quarter left each appear once.
   Check suggested catalogue amounts keep the reminder to check the label.
   A custom food without a known compatible pack must keep unknown/check behaviour.

6. Keyboard and exact amount
   Open Add amount > Exact amount, tap the number to open the iPhone keypad and
   enter 250 g. The complete minus, number, plus and unit controls must remain
   reachable by scrolling, with no vertical clipping. Done must stay above the
   keyboard. Test portrait, landscape and a smaller iPhone if available.
   Dismiss the keypad, then tap count buttons or plus/minus: they must not reopen it.

7. Out and unopened packs
   With no unopened packs, Out shows None at home and hides amount fields.
   With two unopened 1 kg packs, Out must keep 2000 g and show the unopened-pack
   balance. Reopen and repeat Out; it must not erase or duplicate those packs.
   Repeat with two unopened six-packs of eggs and confirm twelve eggs remain.

8. Shopping gap and cooking
   For 500 g needed with 250 g at home, check a 250 g shopping gap before buyable
   pack rounding. With unknown stock, all 500 g remains to buy until checked.
   Cook a test meal and confirm the remaining pantry stock is deducted once.
   Closing, cancelling or reopening the sheet must not change saved stock.

9. Navigation and save safety
   Switch Plan, Discover, At home, Shop and Cook. Check there is no stale screen
   flash or stranded backdrop. Try an offline save, reconnect and confirm one
   absolute quantity is retained. Check a second household device if applicable;
   do not overwrite a partner conflict without reviewing the existing warning.

10. Upload after device checks
    Archive from the prepared workspace, or run the launcher with --archive.
    Confirm archive 1.0.4 (54), then Distribute App > App Store Connect > Upload.
    Do not run ordinary ios:sync. Browser checks do not replace a real phone test.
