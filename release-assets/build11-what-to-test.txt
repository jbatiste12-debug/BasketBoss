BasketBoss build 11 — Weekly essentials in Shop

• At home → Weekly essentials: select an item such as toilet rolls or washing-up liquid and enter Total needed / Already got. Shop should show only the amount still needed.
• In Shop, find Weekly essentials at the bottom of the shopping list. Check the section also appears on the Shop overview and in full-screen Shop Mode.
• Tap a saved essential again, including an item previously marked as owned or skipped. It should return to Shop with the new top-up amount.
• Tick an essential as bought. The item count and remaining estimate should update once. Untick it and confirm the amount returns.
• Finish Shop and check the purchased quantity is added to the household balance once. Food and household stock must stay separate.
• When Already got covers Total needed, the essential should not appear in To buy. Changing the target to need more should bring back only the shortfall.
• Check an essentials-only shop with no meals planned. The list should still be usable.
• Check the final rows stay visible above the bottom navigation and Shop action bar on your phone.

Normal Premium subscriptions and eligible TestFlight Beta Premium access are unchanged.
