BasketBoss Build 14 — Weekly essentials, made clearer

In At home → Weekly essentials, tap a thumbnail or Add.
Enter the total you need this week and what you already have.
Check the amount to buy, then tap Confirm & update Shop. That's it — Shop updates immediately.

Your confirmed list shows what's on Shop and what's already covered at home. Use Amend to change an amount, or Delete to remove the weekly request.

In Shop and Begin shop, essentials now keep their subcategories, including your custom groups. Food quantities stay in At home.

Reviewed receipt prices appear discreetly beneath Kitchen items as Last paid, with the pack, shop and date. These are historical prices; future shop totals remain estimates.

Please test:
• Need 3 packs, already have 1 → Shop should show 2 packs.
• Amend an amount → one updated row, no duplicate.
• Already have enough → nothing extra to buy.
• Add a household item and choose a subcategory → find it under the same group in Begin shop.
• Delete an essential → its weekly Shop request disappears; the amount at home is kept.
• Tick an item only after buying it, then Finish shop as usual.
• After reviewing a receipt, check the Last paid line in Kitchen. Switching regions or accounts must not show another region's or account's prices.

If something looks wrong, send the build number, item name, amounts and a screenshot.
