BasketBoss 1.0.6 (56) — phone checklist

Use your own accepted test household and reversible plan changes. Restore
only your own existing membership; no new purchase is needed for these checks.

1. Prepare and run
   Run the Build 56 launcher. Confirm App version 1.0.6, build 56 and bundle ID
   uk.co.basketboss.app. Connect your iPhone and choose Product > Run.

2. Sharing entry layers
   Open Share with Partner from Account, then from Settings. Sharing should
   replace its entry panel. Close Sharing and confirm no earlier modal or
   backdrop remains stranded over Plan.

3. Automatic dinner updates with Sharing open
   Keep both accepted household apps in the foreground. On one device, make
   a small test dinner change or confirm Auto-fill into eligible empty days.
   Leave the other device's Partner Sharing panel open. Wait for its periodic
   refresh and confirm the changed dinner is received without pressing Sync.
   Close Sharing and confirm Plan displays the received dinner.

4. Automatic dinner updates with Sharing closed
   Repeat with the receiving app already showing Plan. Check the dinner name,
   day and servings update without changing tabs or restarting. Repeat in the
   other direction. Keep both native clients on this update for the check.

5. Edits and cooking remain protected
   Leave an unfinished text entry or cooking screen open on the receiver.
   A shared change must wait, rather than erase the draft or interrupt Cook.
   Finish or close the editor, then return to the foreground and confirm the
   next safe refresh. Conflicting dinner edits must remain available for review.

6. Account and deletion feedback
   Confirm normal sign-in, your existing household role and your own membership
   restoration still work. An old request must not update another account.
   Account-deletion cancellation failures must retain saved data and the
   pending-deletion state; they must not claim success or bypass cancellation.
   Use isolated automated fixtures for destructive failure cases.

7. Pantry, shopping and native controls
   Check a known pantry quantity and its shopping gap before and after sharing.
   Receiving a Plan must not consume stock. Check camera access, native safe
   areas, bottom navigation and keyboard reachability in Add amount.

8. Archive and upload
   Confirm 1.0.6 is the intended App Store Connect version and build 56 is unused.
   Archive from the prepared workspace or rerun the launcher with --archive.
   Confirm Organizer shows BasketBoss 1.0.6 (56), then choose Distribute App >
   App Store Connect > Upload. Upload and App Store submission are manual.
   Do not run ordinary ios:sync after the launcher.

The app refreshes while it is open. Background push updates and instantaneous
delivery are not promised. Browser and mocked archive checks do not replace
these physical-device checks.
