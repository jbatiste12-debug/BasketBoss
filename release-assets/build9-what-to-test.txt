BasketBoss build 9

Your pantry now works as a running balance: shopping adds to it and confirmed cooking uses it up. Remaining food and essentials stay there between weeks.

Please test:
• Welcome tour: read the “A bank account for your kitchen” slide. Returning users can use Settings → Replay welcome & setup. Completing or replaying the tour must keep your pantry and plan intact.
• Compact Saved weeks / Saved meals buttons: open either panel, switch without losing a typed name, then close it to keep the dinner plan visible.
• Confirm cooked: check the amount used, then confirm once. Reopening the meal should not deduct again.
• Day done: confirm the day's planned dinner, lunches and dessert together. Already confirmed meals and linked batch leftovers should not use ingredients twice.
• Shop → Add item to buy: enter an item and the additional quantity you want, even if you already have some at home. Try 4, 500 g, 2 packs or 1 bag (500 g).
• Partial shopping: purchase part of a manual top-up, finish the trip and check that the unpurchased amount carries over.
• Receipts and Finish Shop: check purchased food and household essentials increase once, including after reopening the same receipt.
• Units: try grams/kilograms, millilitres/litres, counted produce and known pack contents. Unknown pack sizes should stay unchanged with a review message.
• Auto-fill: see whether it suggests meals using food already at home while still offering a varied week.
• Reopen the app and plan a new week: your remaining pantry balance should still be there.

Also check the existing Beta Premium unlock, Chef Boss typed and voice replies, and photo scan → meal → matching picture. AI needs an internet connection.

When reporting a problem, include the screen, item or recipe name, quantities before and after, and the steps you took. Please do not include passwords or API keys.
