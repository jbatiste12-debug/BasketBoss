BasketBoss 1.0.4 (52) — phone checklist

1. Prepare and run
   Run the Build 52 launcher on your Mac. Confirm Xcode shows version 1.0.4 and
   build 52 for the App target, then connect your iPhone and choose Product > Run.

2. Add food appearance
   Open At home > Add food. Check the cream sheet, location label, rounded food
   input, Got it / Running low / Out selector, Add amount section and Done button.
   Close using the X and reopen; closing must not add or change food.

3. Typing and autocomplete
   Type a food name slowly, then choose an autocomplete suggestion. The keyboard,
   cursor and input should stay stable, without flashing, jumping or lost text.
   Try switching stock levels, opening Add amount and closing the sheet again.

   Tap Got it, Running low and Out in turn. The selector should stay in place
   and acknowledge each tap. If pack or amount confirmation is needed, it must
   say so before changing the quantity. Try Out > Got it > Done without confirming
   an amount: the sheet must stay open and saved stock must remain unchanged.
   Confirm Full pack, Half pack or an exact amount to resolve that pending choice.

4. Amount review and saving
   Check existing measured amounts remain unchanged unless deliberately edited.
   Expand Add amount when needed and enter a known pack or exact quantity. Any
   required confirmation must remain visible; do not assume an unknown amount
   covers a recipe. Confirm once with Done and check the pantry contains one item.

5. Out and unopened packs
   Check Out shows the existing zero-stock guidance. If unopened packs are already
   recorded, keep their existing review behaviour; selecting Out must not silently
   discard them. Cancel a review and confirm saved stock stays as it was.

6. Sheet dismissal and Plan
   Open and close Add food several times, then return to Plan. Check there is no
   lingering green rectangle or dimmed layer, and that the plan cards can be tapped.
   Tap a meal card's arrow. Its day sheet must appear above the dimmed plan;
   close it with X, then open another day. Background and reopen the app once,
   then repeat the same check.
   Switch between Plan, Discover, At home, Shop and Cook, then repeat rapidly.
   The selected destination should appear without flashing the old cupboard view.

7. Budget label
   Check the Plan amount and "to buy" label stay separate with an empty week and
   with a populated week. Check portrait and landscape; meal counts stay dynamic.

8. Reconciliation unchanged
   Using a known test item, check 500 g required with 250 g confirmed at home leaves
   a 250 g gap before buyable-pack rounding. Confirmed stock covering the full need
   should not force an extra purchase. Keep optional top-ups separate.
   Review any item previously saved after Out > Got it > Done without confirming
   an amount: the old flow could keep it at zero. This update does not guess or
   reconstruct earlier stock balances.

9. AI generation: one controlled check
   With internet available and your existing verified Premium access, review a few
   ingredients and request one meal. Check that a recipe appears before its optional
   picture. If it fails, record the exact safe reference/error text and approximate
   time; do not repeatedly retry because failed requests may use a daily attempt.
   A picture-only failure must leave the recipe readable. Confirm saved pantry
   ingredients and the existing plan are unchanged by generation or a failed request.

10. Before uploading
    Confirm the tests above pass on the phone. Use the same launcher with --archive
    only after testing, or choose Product > Archive in the prepared workspace.
    Verify the archive is 1.0.4 (52) before choosing Distribute App. Do not run
    ordinary ios:sync, and do not report the AI service fixed without a successful
    controlled generation check.
