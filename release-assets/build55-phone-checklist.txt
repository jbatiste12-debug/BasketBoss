BasketBoss 1.0.5 (55) — phone checklist

Use your own test household accounts. Do not create purchases or change live
customer data just to complete these checks. Restore only your own membership.

1. Prepare and run
   Run the Build 55 launcher. Confirm version 1.0.5 and build 55 in the App target,
   connect your iPhone and choose Product > Run. Earlier projects stay intact.

2. Premium owner opens sharing
   In your existing Premium account, open Settings > Household Partner Sharing.
   If the server confirms your sharing eligibility, the invitation screen opens.
   It must not offer another subscription. Confirm your expected owner role.

3. Accurate unavailable and Free states
   If Premium is recognised but sharing is unavailable, check the explanation
   says no additional subscription is needed and offers the appropriate retry
   or sign-in action. Do not enable disabled sharing features for this test.
   A verified Free account can still use the normal upgrade path. Network or
   configuration errors must not be presented as a confirmed Free membership.

4. Invitation, email and in-app notice
   Send an invitation only to your own consenting test account. Confirm the
   delivery result is honest: sent, unavailable or failed. An invite can be
   saved even if server email delivery is unavailable; do not expect an email
   without the server email provider configured. Sign in to the invited account
   and leave the app in the foreground. Check its invitation notice appears.
   A partner with a pending invite must be able to accept without buying Premium.
   After acceptance, confirm the shared household opens and the notice clears.

5. Foreground household updates
   With both test devices signed in to the same accepted household, make a small
   reversible change to your test shopping list. Keep the other app foregrounded
   and wait for its refresh cycle. Confirm it updates without manual reopening.
   Repeat in the opposite direction. Check loading, offline and retry states
   are clear; periodic refresh must not duplicate items or overwrite conflicts.
   Backgrounding the app does not promise instantaneous push or email reminders.

6. Session and account isolation
   While sharing is loading, close its sheet or switch between your own test
   accounts. An older account's invitation or household must not flash into the
   new account, and a closed sheet must not reopen itself. Cancelled work must
   not send an invite. Do not delete a real account for this smoke test; deletion
   boundaries and stale responses are covered by isolated regression fixtures.

7. Navigation and meal details
   Switch Plan, Discover, At home, Shop and Cook repeatedly, including quick
   taps while loading. No retired screen should appear then disappear. Open a
   meal using its arrow, close it and switch tabs. No green backdrop should be
   stranded, and the correct screen must remain usable with its normal controls.

8. Pantry and shopping regression
   Add a test item with Got it and no amount: unknown stock must stay unknown.
   For 500 g needed with 250 g at home, confirm the 250 g shopping gap before
   buyable-pack rounding. Running low, Out and existing unopened packs must
   retain their existing behaviour. Open Add amount with the iPhone keypad:
   all controls remain reachable, Done is visible, and no row is clipped.

9. Native checks
   Check your existing sign-in, own membership restoration, camera access,
   safe areas and bottom navigation. No extra subscription should be required
   for an already recognised Premium account. Never share keys or login links.

10. Upload after device checks
    Confirm 1.0.5 is the intended App Store Connect version and build 55 is unused.
    Archive from the prepared workspace, or run the launcher with --archive.
    Confirm archive 1.0.5 (55), then Distribute App > App Store Connect > Upload.
    Do not run ordinary ios:sync. Browser tests do not replace a real phone test.

PLAN OPTIONS
- Open More options, then Budget & household and Meal preferences. Both should expand within the page and push later controls down.
- Open the keyboard in the budget field, scroll to Set budget, and check no content is clipped.
- Cancel Clear week and confirm all meals remain.
