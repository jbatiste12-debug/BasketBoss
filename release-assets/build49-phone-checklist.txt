BasketBoss 1.0.3 (49) — phone checks

1. Run on your iPhone from the new Xcode workspace; confirm version 1.0.3, build 49.
2. At home: open Rice with a known amount. Tap Got it, then save. The amount
   must stay the same. Close another draft without confirming; stock stays saved
   as it was. Check the editor fits a narrow screen and its controls remain usable.
3. With a confirmed 1kg pack and no spares, Running low should show an estimated
   250g. Add one same-size full spare: it should show 1,250g. Tap Out: the opened
   pack is empty and the full spare still counts as 1,000g. Repeated taps must not
   add more stock. Negative or fractional spare counts must not save.
4. Open Edit amount, enter an exact 175.25g, and save. Reopen: retain that exact
   amount. A missing or incompatible pack size must require review before using
   a level estimate. Change a pack size after estimating: choose a level again or
   enter an exact amount before saving. A stale editor must not overwrite a newer
   saved amount.
5. In Shopping, an estimate should show a compact Check stock prompt. Open it:
   the same editor appears and opening alone does not change the amount. Verify
   500g needed / 250g stocked = 250g to buy. Cooking deducts once; checking an item
   and importing its receipt must not credit the same purchase twice.
6. With existing household access, save a confirmed pack size and check it on the
   linked partner's device. Exact balances must not be credited again by capacity
   sync. Check an offline edit after reconnecting; conflicting edits should ask
   for attention. Switching account or household must not send the old household's
   queued pack metadata into the new household.
7. Confirm the empty and populated At home views, food/receipt scans, saved plans,
   Discover/Plan/Cook recipe pictures, and existing sign-in, household access and
   purchase/restore flows. Reopen a previously viewed recipe offline.
8. Archive, then confirm version 1.0.3 (49) and uk.co.basketboss.app before upload.
   Use the prepared workspace; do not run ordinary ios:sync after preparation.

Signing, an actual device run and Apple upload are Mac checks. Automated Linux
checks do not certify a signed iOS build or a live household session.
