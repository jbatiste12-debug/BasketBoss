BasketBoss 1.0.4 (53) — phone checklist

1. Prepare and run
   Run the Build 53 launcher. Confirm version 1.0.4 and build 53 in the App target,
   connect your iPhone and choose Product > Run. Earlier projects stay intact.

2. Add food default and keyboard
   Open At home > Add food. Only Food, Got it / Running low / Out, the short helper,
   collapsed Add amount and Done should show. Check autocomplete, normal typing
   and the X close button. Opening or closing must not change saved food.
   Open Add amount. Scroll its contents; the header, statuses and Done stay visible.
   Check the first tap on a pack-size chip works, including with the keyboard open.

3. Got it without an amount
   Add a new food, choose Got it and tap Done without opening Add amount. It should
   save once as unknown. If a meal requires it, Shop must retain the full quantity
   to buy and show Check stock. Unknown does not mean zero, a full pack or enough.
   Tap Done twice quickly on a fresh item and confirm only one item is saved.

4. Running low and estimates
   Choose Running low, open Add amount and confirm the actual pack size. Low means
   an estimated quarter of the opened pack. A 1 kg pack should show 250 g remaining
   before adding any unopened packs. Suggested sizes retain Estimated/check-label
   guidance; guessing a size must never silently cover an unconfirmed quantity.

5. Out and unopened packs
   With no unopened packs, Out shows None at home and hides amount fields.
   With one unopened 1 kg pack, Out shows Opened pack finished and one unopened
   pack left. The saved total remains 1000 g. Reopen through autocomplete and make
   sure the unopened-pack count is retained, including after another Out tap.

6. Optional amounts and known stock
   Check Full pack and Half pack each appear once. Use Extra unopened packs ±,
   then Exact amount and Unit. Save an exact amount and reopen to check it.
   Existing saved 250 g stock must remain 250 g unless deliberately changed.
   Clearing a known amount must ask for review instead of silently forgetting it.
   Invalid or unfinished fields must not save an older valid amount.

7. Shopping gap
   For a meal requiring 500 g with 250 g confirmed at home, Shop must show a
   250 g gap before buyable-pack rounding. Full coverage must not force a top-up.
   Repeat with unknown stock: the complete 500 g need stays until checked.

8. New week — cancellation and archives
   Populate a test week. Start next week, then cancel; the plan and pantry must
   remain unchanged. Start again and confirm. The new meal plan should be empty,
   with Auto-fill still available. Check the previous week in Saved weeks and the
   previous shopping trip/unfinished list in its existing history.

9. New week — unfinished days and optional pantry review
   Leave a planned day unfinished and start next week. Choose Keep pantry as it is:
   stock must stay unchanged and the meal must not become Done or cooked.
   Repeat with a disposable test week and choose the optional pantry review. Review
   should open only after the week has successfully changed; stock changes only
   through your deliberate existing pantry controls. Cancelled or failed transitions
   must neither open the review nor deduct any ingredients.

10. Navigation and one controlled AI check
    Switch Plan, Discover, At home, Shop and Cook. Check no old screen flashes or
    stranded dimmed layer. With verified Premium and internet, request one meal.
    If generation fails, record the safe error/reference and time; repeated failures
    may use daily attempts. A picture-only failure should leave the recipe readable.

11. Upload after device checks
    Archive from the prepared workspace, or run the same launcher with --archive.
    Confirm archive 1.0.4 (53), then Distribute App > App Store Connect > Upload.
    Do not run ordinary ios:sync. Linux checks do not replace this device test.
