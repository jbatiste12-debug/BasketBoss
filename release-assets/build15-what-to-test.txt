BasketBoss Build 15 — Good finds. Used first.

New in Premium:
• Choose a supermarket in Shop. Open the small amber Yellow sticker helper for estimated reduction windows.
• Spotted / Not yet shares what is happening at that supermarket chain in your region. Tap Spotted when reduced food is actually available. Live reports expire after 90 minutes; offline reports are labelled On this device.
• In the item drawer or Add item to buy, choose Bought reduced and enter the total paid for that quantity. This week's budget uses the actual price; your usual future price stays unchanged.
• Finish shop to add bought food to Kitchen. Reduced foods appear first in Fridge or Cupboard with Use soon (Reduced).
• Move to Freezer keeps the same quantity and clears urgency. Pantry Auto-fill prefers food marked Use soon while respecting your dietary choices.

Please check a partial purchase, a larger pack, reopening the app and a later receipt. Look for duplicate rows, repeated costs or quantities that change unexpectedly.

The landing page, Premium descriptions and tutorials explain the new flow. Existing Free recipes and normal Premium paywalls remain available as before.

If something looks wrong, send the build number, region, supermarket, item name, entered amounts and a screenshot.
