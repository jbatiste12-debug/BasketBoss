📅 1. Plan — your week at a glance

Overview and Planner are now combined into Plan. Your centred My Week heading, green spending donut, budget editor and all seven dinner days live together in the same polished card.

  1. Beside the green budget donut, enter your weekly grocery target (£) and tap Set budget or Update budget. The ring fills with the percentage of your target represented by estimated planned shopping. When over target, it turns red; when no target is saved, it stays unfilled. Your target is stored on this device until changed.
  2. Tap a day to expand its meal card and see the picture, ingredients and available actions. Expanded days stay open if you switch tabs or edit your plan, until you collapse them.
  3. Tap the single prominent Auto-fill My Week button to run seven varied family dinners, filling eligible empty days only and rotating through recipes you have not previously been offered on this device. Open How Auto-fill works for details; the button respects Free/Premium access and never replaces meals you planned.
  4. ✨ Smart Auto-Fill Pro: Choose Premium pills for under-30-minute dinners, high-protein meal inspiration, or fewer heavily spiced recipes. These are recipe-name and ingredient heuristics, not verified nutrition or suitability guarantees. Free users can preview the benefits without changing their plan.
  5. 🍲 Cook Once, Eat Twice: Switch on Batch Mode, then use Auto-fill when Monday–Saturday are free. It adds a double batch on Monday and Wednesday, linked leftover nights on Tuesday and Thursday, an ordinary Friday family meal and a Saturday family dinner. Sunday stays unfilled in optional Batch Mode. Shopping counts the two double batches once each; Tuesday and Thursday add no new recipe requirements. Cooling, safe storage and reheating still take time. Opening the source dinner shows a double-quantity preparation reminder. Save or restore a backup to keep the linked plan intact.
  6. ✨ Trim My Basket: At 90% of your weekly budget target, a gold Premium banner offers a comparison of one or two replacement dinners. BasketBoss evaluates the actual estimated shopping basket with Pantry deductions; it previews the swaps and estimated saving before you confirm. The comparison never changes your saved plan until you approve. Batch sources are protected.
  7. Use Remove meal inside an expanded dinner, or Clear week beside the dinner plan heading to clear all dinners and desserts after confirmation. This keeps Pantry and school lunches.
  8. Expand School lunches & essentials or More planning actions & favourites as required.

🔎 2. Discover — find recipes

Swipe the large featured recipe to browse ideas (without adding it). Use View Recipe & Cook on that featured view. Scroll to the recipe cards below and tap Add to week on an unlocked card to choose a free day. You can still open the recipe to view or cook it. Locked Premium meals show a faded image with the BasketBoss logo and Unlock with Premium.

🥫 3. Kitchen — your PANTRY

Kitchen has three clear expandable sections—What's in your Cupboard?, What's in your Fridge?, and What's in your Freezer?—followed by one PANTRY list. Open each section to select your foods.

Step 1: Expand each location and tap the foods you have. Add foods that aren't shown from inside that section. Each section header tracks its selected count.
Step 2: Tap View PANTRY or scroll to PANTRY just below the three sections. All selected foods are grouped under Cupboard, Fridge and Freezer, including previously recorded measured stock.
Step 3: Check and edit amounts and units, or remove foods you no longer have. You can leave an amount unknown.
Step 4: Tap Confirm & Reconcile Shopping List. BasketBoss saves your Pantry and opens the Shop tab so you can see the reconciled list of what is still needed.

Only compatible measured amounts are deducted. Unknown amounts never count as enough food, and confirming Pantry does not mark shopping items as bought.

🛒 4. Shop — buy what you need, keep what is left

First, confirm Cupboard, Fridge and Freezer quantities in Kitchen. Compatible measured stock is deducted from this week's shopping requirements. Unknown quantities do not automatically reduce the list.

  1. Open Live Shop and tick Bought beside each item as you pick it up. Tick boxes do not immediately add food to Kitchen.
  2. Tap ••• to choose 📦 Standard pack (where an eligible measured pack is known), ⚖️ Bought exact amount, or ✏️ Enter different amount. Enter at least the quantity still required when recording a different amount. Loose produce never gets a guessed pack remainder.
  3. Choose 🏠 Already have enough if you forgot to record measured food in Kitchen. BasketBoss adds the remaining required amount to Kitchen and removes it from To buy without recording a new purchase or spend. Check your Kitchen quantities afterward.
  4. Finish Shop transfers only confirmed, unused quantities into Kitchen for next week, once per recorded purchase. Fresh food requires you to confirm you can safely store it; always check use-by dates. If you bought exactly the amount needed, nothing extra is credited.

Shop uses six supermarket aisles: Produce, Meat & Fish, Dairy & Chilled, Bakery, Cupboard / Pantry and Household & Other. Portion and Full Pack prices remain estimates; you can edit local shelf prices separately from actual quantities.

The example: 400g needed for the week, 100g already in Kitchen, so 300g remains on To buy. Buy a real 500g pack and confirm it: 200g can be carried to next week after you finish shopping. The 200g does not reduce the current week's requirement a second time.

🍳 5. Cook — Make a Meal

In Make a Meal, use consistent ingredient picture and emoji tiles. Cook now matches Kitchen: expand Cupboard, Fridge and Freezer, choose the foods you have, and review them in one grouped YOUR INGREDIENTS list with editable quantities. Black pepper, mushrooms, carrots and prawns have distinct fallback symbols. Selected foods from Cupboard, Fridge and Freezer appear in a live overview near the picker.

Review amounts in the single ingredient list, then tap Confirm — Make a Meal to show local recipe suggestions. This does not reconcile or change the Shopping stock. Recipe matching can still require extra ingredients, substitutions and fresh quantity checks.

Expand a planned meal to access cooking controls. Dessert cards use the same responsive title, photo and full-sized Cook / Remove buttons as dinner cards. Safety guidance now uses suitable practical checks and pack instructions, while a thermometer remains useful where available. Follow allergy and food-safety guidance carefully.

♛ Free and Premium

Free users can browse the free recipe selection. Premium-only recipe photos appear locked and cannot be added to a new free weekly plan, including through Auto-fill. Premium members can access the expanded library, Smart Auto-Fill Pro filters, Batch Mode and Trim My Basket. These tools use local recipe data and do not call an AI service. Upgrade previews are also shown to Free users.

The Premium planner suite runs locally and uses the existing private QA entitlement toggle for testing. A client-side-only gate is not a secure way to enforce a real paid subscription. Private development QA membership previews are not payment verification. Previously planned Premium meals can remain saved when changing to Free, but are locked until access returns.

Help, account and backups

Open the ⚙ Settings control, then choose the Tutorials tab to expand help for Plan, Discover, Kitchen, Shop or Cook. Choose Settings within that same window for account, backup, region and app controls. Keep a backup of your plan and inventory before switching devices or clearing browser storage.
