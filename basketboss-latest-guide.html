From opening BasketBoss to your first meal

  1. Follow welcome/setup once, or choose to browse the free recipes. Setup and sign-in are optional.
  2. Choose a shopping region and supermarket so units, currency and available estimates match where you shop.
  3. Sign in with your existing account if you want account features. Sign in authenticates your details; Continue without signing in continues setup as a guest.
  4. Use the five bottom tabs. From Plan, open the gear for account, preferences, backups, guides, Privacy, Terms and Delete Account & Data. You can replay welcome/setup from Settings.

Discover · choose your meals

Browse the 100 Free recipes or your verified Premium library. Swipe the featured meal to browse; use Add to week on recipe cards to choose a day. Opening a recipe lets you review ingredients, servings and instructions before planning it.

Tap a recipe in the list to bring its larger preview into view. Recipe highlights show available classifications such as 30 mins or less, Kid-friendly or a Batch-cook option; a label appears only where the recipe record supports it.

Plan · your week and budget

Set a weekly grocery target, choose dinner days and check portions. The budget ring compares your planned shopping with your target. Confirmed reduced purchases use the actual price paid for this week; items still to buy remain estimates. The ring reflects your current region, selected supermarket, plan and compatible Pantry deductions.

Expand a day to cook, replace or remove its meal. Auto-fill fills eligible empty days and keeps meals you already chose. Premium planning tools offer quick-meal filters, batch planning and reviewed swap suggestions. School lunches stay in Plan. Household Weekly essentials are in At home and contribute to the shopping list separately from food stock.

Changing servings scales ingredient quantities before required packs are counted. Estimates use available product guides and reviewed prices; check current shelf prices and pack sizes before buying.

Plan · choose ingredients to avoid

  1. In Plan, tap Ingredients to avoid. The picker stays collapsed until you open it.
  2. Use Find an ingredient to search 59 ingredient families, or browse the food tiles. Tap the ingredients you do not eat or enjoy; tap a selected tile again to allow it.
  3. Tap Done to close the picker. Your choices apply to standard Auto-fill, Premium Auto-fill and Cook from your Pantry planning. Meals you already chose stay in your plan.

Your ingredient choices save with your planner and are included in its backups and explicit cloud upload/restore. Clear all allows every ingredient again for future Auto-fill. Choices match declared recipe ingredients; check recipes and product labels for any allergy or dietary requirement.

Save a named week

In Plan → Saved weeks, enter a name such as Week 1 and tap Save this week. This keeps the dinner days, servings, desserts and school lunches on this device for your current account.

Use week asks before replacing the current plan. Your At home stock stays as it is; old shopping checkmarks are archived. After loading a week, check At home, confirm your quantities, then open Shop for what remains.

A quicker dinner tonight Premium feature

With Premium, when tonight’s plan feels like too much, review a quicker meal you already planned for later in the same week.

  1. Open the current week in Plan and expand tonight’s dinner. Below Cook, tap Too tired / short on time?.
  2. Choose a quicker planned dinner from the sheet. A suggestion needs enough confirmed At home quantities for that meal’s planned servings. If nothing qualifies, keep your plan or check your At home amounts.
  3. Review both meals: the quicker one moves to tonight, and tonight’s original dinner moves to the other day. Read the actual packaging and enter the requested use-by dates for fresh ingredients moving later. The dates must cover that later day.
  4. Confirm the postponed ingredients are suitable for the later date and tonight’s ingredients are ready to cook, including any thawing. Then tap Swap these two meals. Use Keep my plan to leave without saving.

Both meals keep their planned servings, so their recipe quantities for the week stay the same. Swapping does not deduct food from PANTRY; update At home as you use it. Packaging dates are entered for this review, rather than guessed from a receipt.

At home · what have you already got?

Keep food in Cupboard, Fridge or Freezer. Household products stay separate in Weekly essentials.

  1. Tap Add item, a food thumbnail or Edit to open a separate panel. Enter At home and check Total needed. The total starts from your meal plan; increase it for extra stock.
  2. Check Add to Shop, then confirm. If nothing is missing, tap Done. Entries stay in the panel until you confirm; closing it discards unconfirmed edits. The list’s + / − controls still save immediately.
  3. For household products, tap a Weekly essentials thumbnail or Add. Enter Total needed this week and Already at home, check the amount to buy, then tap Confirm & update Shop. There is no second acceptance step.
  4. The confirmed essentials list shows what is on Shop and what is already covered at home. Use Amend to change an amount or Delete to remove the weekly request while keeping recorded stock.
  5. Before the next shop, open Weekly kitchen check → Review & restock. Tap an item to open its separate amount panel, then choose Save & back to check. Each compact row shows At home, Planned including your buffer and To Shop after pack rounding. Confirm an edit to update the same row and Shop.

If you have 2 tins and need 5, confirm to add 3 tins to Shop. Optional Extra stock buffer keeps backups beyond your meal plan. Buy in multiples of rounds only the missing amount to a full pack: 200 ml at home and 333.33 ml needed becomes a 1 L purchase when you choose a 1 L pack. Purchases enter At home when you finish shopping. Compatible stock across Cupboard, Fridge and Freezer counts together. Confirming the same total again keeps one request rather than adding it twice. Existing meal and Shop requirements are kept. Confirmation does not mark anything bought.

Unknown quantities stay saved for review. Reviewed receipt prices appear beneath matching Kitchen items as Last paid, with the pack, shop and date. They describe a past purchase; future totals remain estimates.

Shop · buy what is still needed

Your basic shopping list is Free without a limit. On a phone, choose View full list · Free to see every item. Signed-in Free accounts also get one new advanced shopping trip per week; Premium includes unlimited advanced trips. The Free allowance resets on Monday at 00:00 UTC. Reopening the same unfinished trip does not spend another allowance, including after the week changes.

Confirming the At home panel updates the food shortfall. Household essentials use their confirmed target minus what is already at home. Use Add item to buy for an extra quantity you want to purchase; only the item and quantity are required.

Choose your region and supermarket, then tap Begin shop when ready. Weekly essentials keep the same subcategories as At home. Tap an item’s main circle once to record the displayed amount as bought. Open ••• → Enter different amount for a partial purchase or a larger pack; the remaining shortfall stays on the list.

Finish shop adds the full confirmed purchase to At home once. Confirm safe storage for fresh extras where requested. Cooking then deducts what you use; household goods remain separate from food stock. Already have enough records checked stock rather than a new purchase.

Premium adds Yellow sticker & reduced-food tools and optional receipt scanning. Reviewed receipt totals and logged reduced prices describe actual purchases; unbought items still use estimates.

Yellow stickers & reduced food Premium feature

A good find is more useful when it makes it into a meal. Premium connects the store check, the price you paid and the food to use first.

  1. In Shop, choose your region and supermarket under Where are you shopping?. Open the amber Yellow stickers pill for typical reduction windows in the UK, Guernsey or Jersey. Times are estimates and can vary by branch, opening hours and stock.
  2. At the shop, tap Spotted when reduced stickers are out, or Not yet when they are not. Live reports describe the selected chain in your region and expire after 90 minutes. An offline report is labelled as being on this device.
  3. When buying a reduced item, open its ••• menu and choose Bought reduced. Enter the actual amount paid for the quantity bought, then confirm the purchase. The optional reduced controls are also available in Add item to buy.
  4. When reviewing a receipt, confirm which food lines are reduced and check their prices. A normal promotion or loyalty discount is not automatically a Use soon item. Only confirmed purchases update stock and spending.
  5. After the stock is added, reduced food is pinned first inside Fridge or Cupboard with Use soon (Reduced). If suitable for freezing, tap Move to Freezer: the stock is moved and the urgent badge clears. Follow the product’s storage and use-by instructions.
  6. In Plan, use Autofill empty days using pantry stock to favour meals that use these ingredients first. Your dietary choices and ingredient exclusions still apply; review the suggested meals.

The reduced price updates this week’s budget without replacing your usual price for future weeks. Store reports do not guarantee that a particular branch still has reduced food available.

Scan a receipt Optional Premium feature

In Shop, tap Scan receipt · Premium at the top of your shopping list. After shopping, a receipt can help record what you bought and add reviewed food to At home. It is separate from Cook → Take or choose a photo, which recognises ingredients in a food photo.

  1. Open Shop, check your shopping region and supermarket, then tap Scan Receipt. Sign in to a confirmed Premium account; scanning needs internet access and available daily allowance.
  2. Take or choose a clear receipt photo. On iPhone, allow Camera or Photos access when requested. In the PWA, use your browser’s photo chooser. Crop out personal and payment details. Supported photos must be under 12 MB.
  3. Review the photo and agree to send it to Google Gemini for receipt reading. Tap Scan receipt; complete any security check shown.
  4. Check the supermarket, currency, total, discounts and every suggested line. Correct names, pack amounts, units and prices. Choose where food belongs: Cupboard, Fridge or Freezer. Keep unknown amounts unknown, and leave out anything you do not want to save. Household items and non-food spending stay separate from food stock.
  5. Tap Confirm receipt & add food only when the review is correct. A scan or photo preview alone does not save stock. Confirmed food updates At home; reviewed regular product prices can inform future shopping estimates for that supermarket and region.
  6. Before your next shop, use At home → Weekly kitchen check to review what remains. Confirm the quantity panel to update Shop; compatible stock reduces what you still need to buy.

If confirmation is interrupted, use Check receipt status or the saved-receipt check shown. Recover the original confirmation before scanning or adding the purchase again. Finish Shop may pause while this is checked to prevent duplicate stock.

BasketBoss does not save receipt photos. Reviewed receipt records and food amounts are saved after confirmation; original photos in your own photo library remain yours. Read the privacy policy.

Cook · photo to meal

  1. Tap Take or choose a photo and include the foods you want to use.
  2. Tap Find my ingredients and approve sending the resized food photo for AI recognition.
  3. Check the recognised ingredients. Correct any names and uncheck foods that are not there. Choose up to 12 ingredients.
  4. Tap Generate meal & picture. The AI uses this reviewed list to suggest a meal, then requests an illustrative picture of that recipe.

This meal selection stays separate from At home, Shopping and your saved plan. The At home camera still has its own explicit food and quantity confirmation before updating pantry stock.

AI requires Premium, internet access and available daily allowance. Review the recipe’s quantities, allergens and safe cooking instructions. A picture is a serving illustration, not proof of ingredients, portions or food safety. If it is unavailable, the written recipe stays visible.

While following a recipe, tap Ingredients & prep or the Prep stage to review your ingredients. Continue cooking resumes the same step and keeps checked ingredients and tools.

Voice Cook Optional Premium feature

The Turn on voice control stays available from Prep through Cook. Tap it to begin; it never starts listening automatically. Allow microphone and speech-recognition access if prompted. Voice availability depends on your device and browser, and the on-screen controls remain available.

In Prep, your guide reads one ingredient and its scaled quantity at a time. Gather it, then say “Next ingredient” or “Ready” to mark it ready and hear the next one. Say “Repeat” to hear it again, or “Start cooking” when you want to begin the method. Reading an ingredient alone does not tick its checkbox.

During cooking, the guide reads the full instruction for each step. Say “Next step”, “Previous step” or “Repeat” to control the pace. Ask “How many onions?” or “How much cheddar?” to hear the amount already scaled for this meal. “Start timer” starts the shown step timer; it does not check food safety.

The guide uses the best available installed British system voice at a normal pace. Voice quality depends on the voices available on your device. Tap Stop listening to switch it off. Leaving Cook, backgrounding the app, changing account or losing verified Premium stops the session; tap again to restart. BasketBoss does not store audio or transcripts. Your device’s speech service may require a connection. Read the privacy policy.

The PWA and iPhone app

The five tabs and meal-planning flow are shared. Each installation has its own local storage, so signing in on one does not automatically sign you in on the other.

PWA · browser or Home Screen

Open BasketBoss in your browser or install it from the browser’s Share/menu controls. Use the photo chooser for receipts and food photos.

Reopen the PWA to receive a published update when connected. Available membership choices appear on the website.

iPhone · installed app

The BasketBoss animation leads into first-time setup, then the app. Returning users go to their saved app. Camera and Photos use the iPhone chooser and permission prompts.

Use Settings → Sign in for an existing account. Tap the compact FREE · Upgrade to Premium banner to compare features. Eligible iPhone purchases and Restore Purchases use Apple’s in-app purchase flow; the website uses its own checkout. App updates arrive through an updated installed build.

Using more than one device

Sign in separately with the same account. Keep a backup, and use the explicit cloud upload/restore controls for your saved plan; restoring asks before replacing the current device’s plan. Receipt and eligible household data use their own confirmed cloud records. Do not clear an installation to move your plan.

Free recipes, saved planning and local At home tools can work offline after loading. Sign-in, checking the weekly advanced-trip allowance, cloud records, Premium retrieval, receipt scans, ingredient recognition and AI meal creation need an internet connection.

At home: buy the difference

Open Cupboard, Fridge or Freezer. Start with four common choices, or tap ＋ Add to see the full selection. Weekly essentials is for household items such as toiletries, toilet rolls, cleaning products and nappies.

  1. Total needed: food needs come from your meals and school lunches. Set a higher total for extra groceries. Enter your target for household items.
  2. Already got: enter the amount you actually have, using the same unit.
  3. To buy: BasketBoss calculates the difference, with a minimum of zero.

For example, 600 g of rice needed minus 200 g already at home means 400 g to buy. Tap the large Confirm & Reconcile button near the top. Repeating confirmation refreshes the amounts without adding duplicates.

Add things throughout the week: manual requests remain until you tick them off. Confirm what you bought or review a receipt to update stock. Complete a guided recipe to deduct its known ingredient amounts once for the servings you cooked. If an older leftover-only stock record needs review, confirm the full amount currently at home before marking the meal cooked. At home remains the shared record used by planning and shopping.

Use the compact food or confirmed essentials list inside each section to review details. Unmeasured quantities and incompatible units need your review; they never receive guessed stock credit.

Shopping together

Linked Premium household members can add extra items during the week in Shop → Shared shopping. Each item shows Added by with the member’s name. These are extra quantities to buy alongside your meal-plan list. They stay shared until someone ticks them off; completed items are available in history. Each person signs in with their own linked account.
