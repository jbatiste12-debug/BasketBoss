One connected week

Start wherever you need: check Kitchen first if you already have food, or browse Discover for inspiration. Your plan creates the shopping requirements; confirmed Kitchen quantities reduce them. Shopping and reviewed receipts help you record purchases. Cook turns your chosen ingredients into meal ideas.

  1. 01DiscoverChoose meals you want to cook.
  2. 02PlanChoose days, portions and your budget.
  3. 03KitchenConfirm the food and amounts you have.
  4. 04ShopBuy the missing food; review your receipt.
  5. 05CookMake dinner and use what is left.

For example: your plan needs 400 g of an ingredient and Kitchen confirms you have 100 g, so Shop asks for the remaining 300 g. Unknown quantities do not count as enough stock. A receipt and Finish Shop must not credit the same purchase twice.

From opening BasketBoss to your first meal

  1. Follow welcome/setup once, or choose to browse the free recipes. Setup and sign-in are optional.
  2. Choose a shopping region and supermarket so units, currency and available estimates match where you shop.
  3. Sign in with your existing account if you want account features. Sign in authenticates your details; Continue without signing in continues setup as a guest.
  4. Use the five bottom tabs. From Plan, open the gear for account, preferences, backups, guides, Privacy, Terms and Delete Account & Data. You can replay welcome/setup from Settings.

Discover · choose your meals

Browse the 100 Free recipes or your verified Premium library. Swipe the featured meal to browse; use Add to week on recipe cards to choose a day. Opening a recipe lets you review ingredients, servings and instructions before planning it.

Tap a recipe in the list to bring its larger preview into view. Recipe highlights show available classifications such as 30 mins or less, Kid-friendly or a Batch-cook option; a label appears only where the recipe record supports it.

Plan · your week and budget

Set a weekly grocery target, choose dinner days and check portions. The budget ring shows estimated planned shopping against your target; it is not your bank balance or a live checkout total. The ring reflects your current region, selected supermarket, plan and compatible Pantry deductions.

Expand a day to cook, replace or remove its meal. Auto-fill fills eligible empty days and keeps meals you already chose. Premium planning tools offer quick-meal filters, batch planning and reviewed swap suggestions. School lunches and essentials also contribute to shopping requirements.

Changing servings scales ingredient quantities before required packs are counted. Estimates use available product guides and reviewed prices; check current shelf prices and pack sizes before buying.

Plan · choose ingredients to avoid

  1. In Plan, tap Ingredients to avoid. The picker stays collapsed until you open it.
  2. Use Find an ingredient to search 59 ingredient families, or browse the food tiles. Tap the ingredients you do not eat or enjoy; tap a selected tile again to allow it.
  3. Tap Done to close the picker. Your choices apply to standard Auto-fill, Premium Auto-fill and Cook from your Pantry planning. Meals you already chose stay in your plan.

Your ingredient choices save with your planner and are included in its backups and explicit cloud upload/restore. Clear all allows every ingredient again for future Auto-fill. Choices match declared recipe ingredients; check recipes and product labels for any allergy or dietary requirement.

A quicker dinner tonight Premium feature

With Premium, when tonight’s plan feels like too much, review a quicker meal you already planned for later in the same week.

  1. Open the current week in Plan and expand tonight’s dinner. Below Cook, tap Too tired / short on time?.
  2. Choose a quicker planned dinner from the sheet. A suggestion needs enough confirmed PANTRY quantities for that meal’s planned servings. If nothing qualifies, keep your plan or check your Kitchen amounts.
  3. Review both meals: the quicker one moves to tonight, and tonight’s original dinner moves to the other day. Read the actual packaging and enter the requested use-by dates for fresh ingredients moving later. The dates must cover that later day.
  4. Confirm the postponed ingredients are suitable for the later date and tonight’s ingredients are ready to cook, including any thawing. Then tap Swap these two meals. Use Keep my plan to leave without saving.

Both meals keep their planned servings, so their recipe quantities for the week stay the same. Swapping does not deduct food from PANTRY; update Kitchen as you use it. Packaging dates are entered for this review, rather than guessed from a receipt.

Kitchen · know what you already have

  1. Open Cupboard, Fridge or Freezer. Select your foods or add an item.
  2. Review the grouped Pantry list. Enter amounts and units you can confirm; leave uncertain quantities unknown.
  3. Choose Confirm & Reconcile Shopping List to update the quantities still required in Shop.

Receipt-confirmed foods appear in Kitchen. Use or edit quantities as food changes, especially before the next shop. Kitchen ownership and ticking a Shop item as Bought are different actions.

Shop · buy what is still needed

Review your region and supermarket, then enter Shop Mode for the aisle list. Tick Bought as items enter your basket. Where offered, use the item menu to record a standard pack, exact amount or a different actual quantity. Use Already have enough only after checking your stock.

Finish Shop transfers only eligible, confirmed unused amounts into Kitchen once. Fresh food needs your safe-storage confirmation. A tick alone does not invent a pack size or immediately add food to Kitchen.

Use the optional receipt scanner below to review a real purchase. Receipt totals are actual recorded spending; the plan’s grocery total remains an estimate. Food and non-food lines are separated.

Scan a receipt Optional Premium feature

After shopping, a receipt can help record what you bought and add reviewed food to Kitchen. It is separate from Cook → Scan my food, which recognises ingredients in a food photo.

  1. Open Shop, check your shopping region and supermarket, then tap Scan Receipt. Sign in to a confirmed Premium account; scanning needs internet access and available daily allowance.
  2. Take or choose a clear receipt photo. On iPhone, allow Camera or Photos access when requested. In the PWA, use your browser’s photo chooser. Crop out personal and payment details. Supported photos must be under 12 MB.
  3. Review the photo and agree to send it to Google Gemini for receipt reading. Tap Scan receipt; complete any security check shown.
  4. Check the supermarket, currency, total, discounts and every suggested line. Correct names, pack amounts, units and prices. Choose where food belongs: Cupboard, Fridge or Freezer. Keep unknown amounts unknown, and leave out anything you do not want to save. Household items and non-food spending stay separate from food stock.
  5. Tap Confirm receipt & add food only when the review is correct. A scan or photo preview alone does not save stock. Confirmed food updates Kitchen; reviewed purchase records and exact product prices can inform shopping estimates for that supermarket and region.
  6. Before your next shop, use Confirm Your Pantry to check what remains, then Confirm pantry & update shopping. Only compatible measured stock reduces what you still need to buy.

If confirmation is interrupted, use Check receipt status or the saved-receipt check shown. Recover the original confirmation before scanning or adding the purchase again. Finish Shop may pause while this is checked to prevent duplicate stock.

BasketBoss does not save receipt photos. Reviewed receipt records and food amounts are saved after confirmation; original photos in your own photo library remain yours. Read the privacy policy.

Cook · make the most of your ingredients

Add food lets you choose ingredients from Cupboard, Fridge and Freezer. Review their quantities before asking for recipe-library matches. This is a cooking selection; it does not automatically change your shopping list.

Scan my food uses an optional Premium food photo to suggest ingredients you review. Create a meal uses your selected ingredients to request an AI recipe draft. Check allergies, amounts, substitutions and cooking safety before using it.

The created recipe appears first. An illustrative AI meal picture can arrive afterward; it is an illustration, not evidence of ingredients, portions or safe cooking. If a picture is unavailable, the written recipe remains usable. Saving or adding a meal uses its own explicit review/confirmation.

Voice Cook Optional Premium feature

The Turn on voice control stays available from Prep through Cook. Tap it to begin; it never starts listening automatically. Allow microphone and speech-recognition access if prompted. Voice availability depends on your device and browser, and the on-screen controls remain available.

In Prep, your guide reads one ingredient and its scaled quantity at a time. Gather it, then say “Next ingredient” or “Ready” to mark it ready and hear the next one. Say “Repeat” to hear it again, or “Start cooking” when you want to begin the method. Reading an ingredient alone does not tick its checkbox.

During cooking, the guide reads the full instruction for each step. Say “Next step”, “Previous step” or “Repeat” to control the pace. Ask “How many onions?” or “How much cheddar?” to hear the amount already scaled for this meal. “Start timer” starts the shown step timer; it does not check food safety.

The guide uses an available system voice, with a relaxed British voice where supported. Tap Stop listening to switch it off. Leaving Cook, backgrounding the app, changing account or losing verified Premium stops the session; tap again to restart. BasketBoss does not store audio or transcripts. Your device’s speech service may require a connection. Read the privacy policy.

The PWA and iPhone app

The five tabs and meal-planning flow are shared. Each installation has its own local storage, so signing in on one does not automatically sign you in on the other.

PWA · browser or Home Screen

Open BasketBoss in your browser or install it from the browser’s Share/menu controls. Use the photo chooser for receipts and food photos.

Reopen the PWA to receive a published update when connected. Available membership choices appear on the website.

iPhone · installed app

The BasketBoss animation leads into first-time setup, then the app. Returning users go to their saved app. Camera and Photos use the iPhone chooser and permission prompts.

Use Plan → Settings → Sign in for an existing account. This iPhone build shows account status and does not offer purchases or new-account creation. App updates arrive through an updated installed build.

Using more than one device

Sign in separately with the same account. Keep a backup, and use the explicit cloud upload/restore controls for your saved plan; restoring asks before replacing the current device’s plan. Receipt and eligible household data use their own confirmed cloud records. Do not clear an installation to move your plan.

Free recipes, saved planning and local Kitchen tools can work offline after loading. Sign-in, cloud records, Premium retrieval, receipt scans, ingredient recognition and AI meal creation need an internet connection.

Help whenever you need it

Open Plan → Settings → Guides for section help, the live app walkthrough and this guide. The walkthrough points at your real controls and does not add meals, scan a photo, confirm purchases or change your budget.
