# Recipe photo audit — 2 November 2026

All 500 primary recipe photos were decoded and visually inspected in labelled contact sheets. The current 100 public records and the authenticated current 400-record Premium snapshot supplied their names. The images flagged below were also compared with the current recipe ingredient names. Image appearance cannot establish exact weights, cooking safety or a kitchen-tested result.

There were no broken WebP files or exact duplicate primary images. Thirteen recipe presentations warranted correction or improvement. Five new AI-generated photographs use the listed foods, natural light, varied viewing angles and a wider 4:3 frame; eight existing photographs were reassigned to the correct dish. Other recipe photos are unchanged.

## Corrected assignments

The image files in each pair had been assigned to the opposite dish. Their bytes were swapped into the correct existing filenames, preserving the recipe URLs and access rules:

- Beef and tomato macaroni ↔ chicken, broccoli and potato rosti bake.
- Creamy cauliflower chicken bake ↔ spinach and cheese cannelloni.
- Sausage and apple casserole ↔ sweet potato and black bean enchiladas.
- Ginger chicken and carrot noodle broth ↔ tuna and sweetcorn pasta fritters.
