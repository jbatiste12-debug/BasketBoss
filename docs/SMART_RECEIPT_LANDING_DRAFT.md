Proposed feature card:

**Your receipt, ready for Kitchen**

Scan your shopping receipt, review the matched items, prices and quantities, then confirm what you brought home. BasketBoss adds the purchases you approve to Kitchen and remembers the prices you confirm for future shop estimates.

Proposed action: **See how SmartReceipt works**

Proposed FAQ:

**Does a receipt scan change my Kitchen stock automatically?**

You review the matches and quantities before confirming them. Only the purchases you approve are added to Kitchen.
