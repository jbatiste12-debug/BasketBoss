Privacy Notice

How your email, plans, pantry and receipt photos are handled — and how to delete your data.

Last updated 1 November 2026.

Privacy in brief

  • Your email identifies your account. Supabase handles sign-in and account authentication.
  • Your plans start on your device. Cloud sync and partner sharing save relevant app data to your account or household.
  • Receipt scanning is optional. You consent before a photo is sent through Cloudflare to Google Gemini, then review the results before saving.
  • BasketBoss does not store receipt photos. Approved item quantities, prices and spending records are saved when you confirm.
  • You can delete your account in Settings. The confirmation explains the permanent deletion and shared-household effects.

1. Who handles your information?

BasketBoss is operated by Jason Batiste from Guernsey, Channel Islands. For privacy requests, contact privacy@basketboss.co.uk. For app support, contact support@basketboss.co.uk.

2. Accounts and email

When you create an account on the web or sign in, Supabase processes your email, account identifier and authentication information. Your password is sent securely to Supabase for authentication; it is not used as an app preference or included in meal plans. BasketBoss uses verified account information to provide cloud access, protect your account and check access to Premium features.

Cloudflare D1 stores app records needed to verify membership, subscription status and usage allowances. Supabase supports verification of the current permitted Premium session. An account identifier connects these records to your account. BasketBoss does not store complete payment-card numbers.

3. Meal plans, pantry and local storage

Meal plans, servings, school-lunch selections, shopping lists, pantry stock, budgets, favourites, custom recipes and preferences can be stored using localStorage and other device storage. The iOS app has storage separate from Safari and the installed PWA. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or browser profile may be able to access local data.

When you choose cloud upload or restore, or use enabled account and household features, relevant meal-plan and shopping data is saved in Supabase. Partner sharing can make meals, portions, pantry quantities, receipt spending and shopping updates visible to your linked partner while their access is active. You do not need to provide children’s names for school-lunch planning.

Cloud access is authenticated, but BasketBoss does not describe this service as end-to-end encrypted. Clearing browser data or removing the iOS app can remove local copies. Exported backups remain files under your control.

4. Receipt photos and AI processing

The Premium receipt scanner can use your camera or a photo you select. Camera and photo-library access are requested when needed. Before an AI upload, the app asks for your permission to send the selected photo through Cloudflare to Google Gemini to read the receipt. You may cancel without uploading.

Photos are processed transiently for the requested scan. BasketBoss does not store receipt photos in its app database or intentionally save them to your photo library. Local previews and temporary device files may exist while you use the scanner. Google’s processing and retention are governed by its service terms and the provider configuration; we do not promise zero retention by external providers. See Google’s Gemini API terms.

After scanning, you review the product matches, food quantities, pack sizes, prices, supermarket, currency and total. Only after confirmation does BasketBoss save approved receipt metadata to Supabase for your account or shared household. Food updates measured pantry stock; household goods remain separate. Reviewed regular prices can help future estimates for matching products, packs, shops and regions.

Crop or obscure addresses, card details and other unnecessary personal information before uploading. AI suggestions can be wrong. Always review them before confirming.

Meal ideas and pictures: if you approve creating an AI meal, BasketBoss sends the selected ingredient names through Cloudflare to Google Gemini for a recipe draft. The resulting recipe details can then be sent to Gemini to create an illustrative picture of that meal. Your account email and original camera photos are not included in the meal-picture request. Generated meal pictures are held temporarily in the current app view; BasketBoss does not save them to its cloud database or your photo library. Provider processing remains subject to the terms above. Pictures are illustrations, not evidence of exact portions, ingredients, allergens or safe cooking.

5. Other services and technical information

  • Cloudflare hosts the website and API and may process normal connection information, such as IP addresses, request timing and security logs.
  • Supabase provides account authentication and cloud data storage.
  • Google Gemini processes optional AI requests that you submit, including approved photo uploads.
  • Paddle processes web subscription payments as merchant of record, including checkout, invoices, taxes and refunds. It retains its own transaction records under its legal obligations.
  • Sharing, feedback and voice input: if you send a shopping list, open an external feedback form such as Tally, or use device speech recognition, the selected provider handles information under its own rules.
  • Anonymous usage steps: limited first-party requests count interactions such as opening Free recipes or starting registration. The event does not include an email, account ID, plan content, recipe identity, tracking cookie or persistent visitor identifier. Cloudflare may still process normal network information. Counts are aggregated requests, not unique visitor counts.

These providers may process information in countries outside your own. Applicable provider terms and safeguards govern their processing. Contact us for questions about the services used for your account.

6. Why we use information

Information is used to provide the app functions you request, calculate shopping needs, remember plans, synchronise permitted household data, secure accounts, enforce usage limits and administer subscriptions. Optional photo processing is initiated with your consent. Account and billing administration may also be needed to perform our service contract, meet legal obligations and protect the service against abuse.

7. Delete Account & Data

Use Delete Account & Data in your account/settings view. You must confirm the permanent action before deletion starts. The app removes your account and associated saved app data from its account, Supabase and D1 systems, revokes access and clears BasketBoss data on the current device once the server confirms completion. The confirmation explains what happens to a linked household and any existing web subscription.

Deletion can require several provider operations. If it cannot finish, the app reports that deletion is incomplete so you can retry; it should not claim success while a required step remains. Minimal deletion-processing records may be kept only as needed to complete the request and prevent deleted-account records from being recreated.

Deleting an account does not remotely erase exported backups, lists you sent to others, or local app/browser copies on another device. Clear BasketBoss app/site data on those devices separately. A linked partner’s own account and independently held data are separate from your account.

Paddle and other payment providers may retain invoices and transaction records required for accounting, tax, fraud prevention or legal obligations. Any minimal records BasketBoss must retain for those purposes are limited to the relevant obligation. You can also contact privacy@basketboss.co.uk for privacy help or to exercise your rights.

8. Retention and security

Local data remains until you clear it, remove the app or use the in-app deletion flow. Saved account data is kept while needed to provide the account and its requested features, subject to deletion and legal requirements. Technical, security and provider records may follow different retention periods.

We use HTTPS for network requests, authenticated cloud access and server-side access checks. No app can guarantee absolute security. Protect your devices and backups, and avoid entering unnecessary sensitive information into notes, photos or feedback.

9. Your rights and contact

Depending on the law that applies, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent or complain to a data protection authority. Contact privacy@basketboss.co.uk; identity verification may be needed to protect your information.

We will update this notice when processing changes and show the revised date. Material changes will be communicated where required.
