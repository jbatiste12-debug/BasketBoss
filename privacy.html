Privacy in brief: BasketBoss is designed to keep your meal plans, lunchboxes, pantry stock and shopping lists in this browser/device in the current local-first version. Choosing to share a list, submit feedback, use an external scanner or enable cloud features can send information outside the device. Those activities are optional and should be clearly identified.

1. Who operates BasketBoss?

BasketBoss is operated by Jason Batiste under the name BasketBoss.

Data controller contact: privacy@basketboss.co.uk. General support: support@basketboss.co.uk.

2. Information you choose to enter

You may enter planned meals, household serving counts, school-lunch selections, shopping lists, purchased-item ticks, food budgets, pantry quantities, recipes, favourites, approximate or self-recorded prices, photo selections and related preferences.

You do not need to enter children's names for lunchbox planning. Please avoid putting unnecessary sensitive personal information into notes, photos, shopping items or feedback forms.

3. Where your information is stored

The current local-first application uses browser storage (including localStorage) for saved plans and settings. This is device/browser and website-origin storage, not an encrypted personal vault. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or browser profile might access stored information.

Clearing site data, using private browsing or removing the app can erase locally stored plans. Backups that you export are files you are responsible for storing securely. Data saved under the same website origin may be available to other BasketBoss previews on that origin.

The app is not yet a guaranteed end-to-end encrypted, multi-device synchronisation service.

4. When information may leave your device

  • Shopping list sharing: Only when you choose Copy, Share or WhatsApp and then send/paste the list. The receiving app or person handles the shared contents under their own rules.
  • Feedback: Choosing the external BasketBoss feedback form opens Tally; information you submit is processed by that provider. Its forms may log technical information when opened.
  • Photo scanner: When Premium AI is enabled, a photo you expressly submit is sent through Cloudflare to Google Gemini for processing. Review suggested ingredients before saving them.
  • Voice input: If you choose speech recognition, your browser/device speech service may process audio or a transcript; BasketBoss does not promise processing occurs solely on the device.
  • Cloud accounts: When you sign in, Supabase processes account information such as your email and account ID. Optional cloud features may process information you choose to save.
  • External recipe photos and websites: If you use a remote image URL or open an outside site, your browser may contact that host and reveal normal connection information such as your IP address. The app currently includes an external image fallback.
  • Anonymous usage steps: We count limited site interactions such as opening the Free recipes, viewing recipes and starting account registration. Each step makes a first-party request to Cloudflare hosting, with no email, password, recipe identity, plan contents, account ID, tracking cookie or persistent visitor identifier supplied in the event. Normal network information (including IP address) may still be processed by Cloudflare. These request counts are aggregated, are not unique visitor counts and may include automated traffic. This helps us improve the app.
  • Hosting: The website/hosting provider may receive normal web requests and technical connection logs even when your meal plan is not uploaded.
  • Membership billing (only when activated): Paddle processes checkout, payment details, invoices, tax and refunds as merchant of record. BasketBoss stores account-to-subscription linkage, plan, trial or cancellation status and related event identifiers in Cloudflare D1 so access can be verified. Payment card numbers are not stored by BasketBoss. Paddle's own privacy notice explains its processing.

BasketBoss uses Cloudflare website metrics and limited first-party anonymous funnel step counts. The funnel endpoint does not set its own cookies or request personally identifying details. No third-party advertising pixel is installed by this change. Please contact the privacy address above with any questions about these metrics.

5. Why information is used

Locally saved information allows BasketBoss to calculate shopping requirements, estimate grocery costs, remember your choices, show pantry coverage, display meal plans and keep your shopping checklist available when you return. Optional external features work only when requested or enabled by the user, subject to their own service settings.

We process account information to provide requested services and secure accounts. Billing records, when enabled, are used to administer subscriptions and meet legal obligations. Retention depends on the category of information and applicable requirements; contact us for details.

6. Your choices, backups and deletion

  • Use Save my BasketBoss backup in the app before clearing data or changing devices.
  • To delete locally stored BasketBoss information, clear this site's data using your browser/device privacy settings. This can remove all meal plans, lunchbox selections, stock quantities and custom tiles for that site.
  • Deleting browser data does not delete copies you previously shared, exported or submitted to outside services; contact the relevant provider/recipient for those.
  • For account-data access or deletion requests, email privacy@basketboss.co.uk. Identity verification may be required.

7. Security and data protection

BasketBoss aims to minimise personal information, store the current plan locally, require user actions for optional sharing and require confirmation before a configured photo-scanner upload. This Privacy & Legal page contains no external JavaScript or tracking pixels and has a restrictive browser content-security policy.

No app can guarantee that information will never be disclosed. Browser storage is not by itself encrypted end to end. A production security review must cover HTTPS hosting, server permissions, secure cloud access, secrets management, external integrations, updates, backup handling and incident response.

You may also raise concerns with the relevant data protection authority under applicable law.
